Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ReadyPack, developed and published by Sahar Yehezkel Avraham, handles information when you use the app.

App: ReadyPack
Android package: com.sahar.readypack
iOS bundle identifier: com.sahar.readypack
Developer: Sahar Yehezkel Avraham

Information handled by the app

The app can save preferences and content you create on your device so its features work as expected. Sahar Yehezkel Avraham does not operate an app account system or a server that receives this saved app content.

The app does not require your name, postal address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, payment information, or login credentials.

Advertising and related data

ReadyPack uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Depending on device settings, consent choices, and region, Google and its advertising partners may process approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Advertising requests are delayed until the applicable consent flow allows them. You can review or change available advertising consent choices from the Privacy section inside the app and through your device settings. The developer does not receive a database containing this advertising information.

Google handles this information under its own policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

Sharing and service providers

The developer does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the services identified above when those services are enabled or when you choose to open an external link. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or reasonably necessary to prevent fraud, abuse, or security incidents.

Security

Reasonable safeguards are used for app-controlled information. Internet transmission and electronic storage cannot be guaranteed to be completely secure, and third-party services protect information according to their own policies.

Retention and deletion

Content and settings saved on your device remain there until you remove them through available app controls, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. The developer does not retain a server copy of that content.

For deletion instructions, developer requests, third-party information, and possible legal retention, see the ReadyPack Data Deletion page.

Children

ReadyPack is not directed to children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an app account or developer-operated server.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when the app, its service providers, or applicable requirements change. The current policy will remain available at this address.
