Email Sahar014g@gmail.com with your device model, operating-system version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful when available.

How Name Mixing Works

NameMash uses algorithmic syllable segmentation, phonetic affinity scoring, and natural cadence blend filters to create names. If you prefer specific prefixes or suffixes, you can explore hundreds of variations across the 4 blending modes (Couple, Baby, Pet, and Ship).

Advertisements

The app contains banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads to support ongoing development and keep the app free. Ad availability and presentation can vary based on your network connection, consent settings, and regional demand.

Saved Combinations & Privacy

All saved combinations and favorite name lists are stored locally on your device. NameMash does not require an account and never uploads your personal names or creations to external servers.

Story Card Export

When creating social story cards, NameMash requests permission to save image cards to your photo library. You can also share them directly to social messaging apps using your device's native share sheet.

Data Deletion

Because NameMash does not operate an account server or store personal data, uninstalling the app or clearing its data permanently removes all stored preferences and saved lists. For any privacy requests, email the support address above.
