RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker

Data Deletion

This page explains how users of RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker, Android package com.sahar.riselog and iOS bundle identifier com.sahar.riselog, can delete app data or request deletion. The app is developed by Sahar Yehezkel Avraham.

RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker does not create user accounts and does not operate a developer database containing content saved inside the app.

Delete data stored on your device

  1. Use any delete, clear, or reset control available inside RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker for content you created.
  2. On Android, you can clear the app's storage from the device's Apps settings.
  3. Uninstall RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker to remove its remaining app data from the device. Device backups remain subject to Apple or Google's backup settings and retention practices.

Request deletion from the developer

Email Sahar014g@gmail.com with the subject “RiseLog: Sourdough Tracker Data Deletion Request.” Include whether you used Android or iOS and briefly describe the information you want deleted. Do not send passwords, payment information, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Sahar Yehezkel Avraham will acknowledge and process verifiable requests within the period required by applicable law. Information may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or another lawful purpose.

Third-party information

Information processed independently by Google and its advertising partners is governed by those providers' privacy and retention policies. Use the app's available privacy controls, your provider accounts, and your device settings to manage applicable choices.
