App Support

Help for Birthday Cake Ideas: CakeMuse on iPhone, iPad, and Android.

Contact support
Email Sahar014g@gmail.com with the device model, operating-system version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful when available.

YouTube tutorials do not load

Check your internet connection and try again. Tutorial results depend on YouTube service availability and may be temporarily limited by service quotas or regional availability.

Advertisements

The app contains banner and occasional interstitial ads. Availability and presentation can vary by region, device settings, consent state, and network connection.

Saved cakes

Saved cakes are stored locally on your device. They are not associated with an account and do not sync between devices. Removing the app or clearing its storage can remove saved cakes.

Requesting deletion

The app has no account and CakeMuse does not receive your locally saved cake list. Remove individual saved cakes in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its local storage. For a privacy request concerning CakeMuse, email the support address above.
