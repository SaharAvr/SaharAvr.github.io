Data Deletion

This page explains how users of Subwoofer Box Calculator, package identifier com.subwooferbox.calculator, can delete app data or request deletion. The app is developed by Sahar Yehezkel Avraham.

No account is required.
Subwoofer Box Calculator does not create user accounts and does not operate a developer database containing your saved enclosure builds.

Delete data stored on your device

  1. Open Subwoofer Box Calculator.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select “Delete All Saved Builds” and confirm “Delete All.”

To remove all remaining local app settings and data, uninstall Subwoofer Box Calculator. Device-platform backups may remain subject to Apple or Google's backup settings and retention practices.

Request deletion from the developer

To request deletion of data associated with Subwoofer Box Calculator that may be controlled by the developer, email Sahar014g@gmail.com with the subject “Subwoofer Box Calculator Data Deletion Request.”

Include the platform you used, Android or iOS, and a short description of the data you want deleted. Do not send passwords, payment information, or other unnecessary sensitive information. Sahar Yehezkel Avraham will acknowledge and process verifiable requests within the period required by applicable law. Information may be retained only when required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or another lawful purpose.
